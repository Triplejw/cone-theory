# Asset authorship and licenses

## Cone Theory brand assets

The Cone Theory concept, UI/UX, visual identity, logo, wordmark, original
design assets, colour palette, and geometric art direction were created by
[Daniel Dencil](https://www.linkedin.com/in/daniel-dencil/). These materials
remain project brand assets and are not offered for unrelated reuse.

The production website, responsive implementation, interaction system,
accessibility work, automated verification, repository workflow, and Vercel
delivery were created by [Joshua JJ Wonder](https://github.com/Triplejw).

The collaborator portraits in `docs/assets/collaborators/` were supplied for
project attribution in this repository. They are not licensed for unrelated
reuse or redistribution.
